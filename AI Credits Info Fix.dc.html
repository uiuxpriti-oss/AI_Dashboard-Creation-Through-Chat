Bug fix · Ask Me usage indicator Explain what AI credits are The Usage popover showed figures only, and the trigger had no accessible name. The fix adds a hover/focus tooltip, an (i) explanation per meter, “used” labelling with credits left and reset date, and proper ARIA. Every frame is live — hover, click and press (i).
Before Current (fails AC)
Figures only. “360.43 / 1.00K” doesn’t say used or left. No title, no aria-label — screen readers hear “Usage”.
1a Hover or focus the pill
Tooltip gives the figure with direction (“used”) and a one-line meaning. Same text is the accessible description.
1b Popover open
Totals read “/ 1,000 used” (no “1.00K”). A meta line under each bar gives what’s left and when it resets. (i) sits next to each label.
1c “What are AI credits?” expanded
Answers the three gaps: what a credit is, what uses one, and what happens at the limit.
1d External tool calls expanded
Same pattern for the second meter. One explanation open at a time.
1e Running low (≥ 90%)
Bar turns orange, meta reads “Only 38.5 left”, and a dot on the pill signals it without opening.
1f Out of credits
States the consequence plainly and promotes Upgrade to the primary button.
Spec Accessibility & copy
TRIGGER · [data-testid="chat-header-credit-pill"] aria-label “Usage: 360.43 of 1,000 AI credits used, 639.57 left. Resets Oct 1.” aria-haspopup="dialog" · aria-expanded · aria-describedby → tooltip Tooltip opens on hover and keyboard focus. Esc or click closes it. No native title, so it doesn’t show twice.
POPOVER role="dialog" aria-label="Usage and limits". Each bar is a progressbar with valuenow/valuemax. (i) buttons: aria-label="What are AI credits?", aria-expanded, aria-controls → explanation.
THRESHOLDS Under 90% blue · 90–99% orange + pill dot · 100% red + filled Upgrade. Numbers use full values (“1,000”), not “1.00K”.
